Concern selection (general/recent sun exposure/uneven pigmentation) changes the introduction and order of information, not diagnosis or a photo-based treatment. No skin-colour analysis, tan score, natural-colour correction, medication or fixed clearing promise. Recommend professional assessment for persistent or concerning changes.

Optional hairstyle, beard-edge and photographic presentation ideas are explicitly FaceFrame styling suggestions. They affect visible framing, not measured anatomy. AAO is the source for the DIY-reshaping caution, not evidence that a particular hairstyle or beard makes a jaw better. Facial-hair suggestions require explicit opt-in; no gender, ethnicity or health is inferred. No weight-loss prescription from a photograph. External sources open on visitor action with noreferrer and no report/photo query parameters. All care choices remain session memory.
